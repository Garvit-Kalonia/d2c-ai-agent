D2C Store Customer Policy

RETURNS

Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery.

To be eligible for a return:

The product must be unused.

The product must be in its original packaging.

The product must not show signs of damage or use.

The customer must provide the order ID.

Return requests are subject to inspection and approval. Submitting a return request does not guarantee that the return will be approved.

REFUNDS

Once a returned product has been inspected and the return is approved, the refund will be processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the order.

The store does not promise an immediate refund when a return request is submitted.

SHIPPING

Standard shipping normally takes 3-7 business days.

Customers receive tracking information after their order has been shipped.

Shipping times are estimates and may vary depending on the order and delivery location.

WARRANTY

Electronics products include a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover:

Accidental damage

Liquid damage

Normal cosmetic wear

Warranty coverage does not override the return policy or guarantee that every reported issue qualifies for a warranty claim.

CANCELLATIONS

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled through the standard cancellation process.

Cancellation requests are subject to the order's current status. If an order has already moved to another status, the cancellation may no longer be available.

D2C Store Customer Policy

RETURNS

Customers may return unused products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be returned in their original packaging and must not show
signs of use or damage.

Customers should provide their order ID when requesting a return.

REFUNDS

Once a returned product has been inspected and the return is approved,
the refund will be processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

SHIPPING

Standard shipping normally takes 3-7 business days.

Customers receive tracking information after their order has been shipped.

WARRANTY

Electronics products include a 12-month limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or
normal cosmetic wear.

CANCELLATIONS

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled through
the standard cancellation process.
